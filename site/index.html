Coverage comes first

Email checks are not bank syncs.

Gmail access is read-only. Email alerts can be delayed, missing or unsupported. Android may delay background checks beyond their 15-minute interval.

Current release supports synthetic parser fixtures only. Real-bank email templates require validation before activation. The preview does not offer universal bank coverage or live verified balances.

Keep the details close.

Encrypted local records, device authentication, and explicit exports put you in control. Optional usage analytics is off by default.

Connect only what you choose.

Phone verification signs you into MoneyTrack. It does not connect bank accounts. Authorized bank-data linking is a future integration.
